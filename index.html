Wheelwave

Turn your old iPhone into your favourite music player.

A click wheel, simple menus and your own music library. Your music, the classic way.

Free · Coming to the App Store

Features

  • Click wheelCircle your thumb to scroll, with adjustable sensitivity, haptics and optional click sounds.
  • Your libraryBrowse playlists, artists, albums, songs, genres and recently added music, or search with the wheel.
  • Now PlayingAlbum art, progress, shuffle and repeat. Use the wheel for volume or to scrub through a song.
  • SpeakersChoose AirPlay and Bluetooth outputs from the system picker.
  • Make it yoursFour device colours and four screen themes, including Night and Mono LCD.
  • Old iPhones welcomeRuns on iOS 15 and later, including the iPhone 6s, iPhone 7 and original iPhone SE.

A closer look

Screens show a sample library with made-up artists and songs.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 15 or later.
  • Music in your library: synced, purchased or downloaded, or from Apple Music if you subscribe.
  • No account, subscription or advertising from Wheelwave. It's free.
